Vocalis

Privacy

Last updated 14 October 2026. Vocalis is Anvit Tewarika's personal voice assistant. Please use fictional contact details when exploring the portfolio site.

Conversations

If you start a voice conversation, your browser sends microphone audio to the Vocalis backend on Render. Groq transcribes speech and generates replies; Edge TTS produces spoken responses. Vocalis does not save a microphone recording. It stores conversation text, call state, and diagnostic events in Neon. Call data is deleted after 30 days by default. Backups, if created, may have separate retention periods.

Meeting requests

If you request a meeting, Vocalis stores your name, phone number, service, and requested time. Confirmed bookings are written to the dedicated Vocalis calendar with your name, service, and meeting time. Calendar events include reminders one day and two hours before the meeting. Booking and lead records are not covered by the automatic call-data deletion process.

Google Calendar access

The owner authorized Vocalis to view and manage events on calendars they own, so it can check for conflicts. Vocalis writes new bookings only to the dedicated Vocalis calendar. Visitors do not sign in with Google or grant access to their calendars.

Questions

For questions or data removal requests, contact the project owner through the Vocalis GitHub repository. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue.
