Privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026. Vocalis is a public engineering demo. Please use fictional names and contact details when trying it.

What the demo processes

If you start a voice conversation, your browser sends microphone audio to the Vocalis backend on Render. The hosted demo uses Groq to transcribe speech and generate replies, and a speech provider to produce spoken responses. It does not save a recording of your microphone audio. It stores conversation text, call state, and diagnostic events in a Neon database. Primary call data is deleted after 30 days by default; private database backups, if any, may have separate retention periods.

If you submit a booking, the demo stores the name, phone number, service, and requested time. Once Google Calendar is connected, it will also write the name, service, and time to a separate private demo calendar. Leads and appointments are not covered by the automatic call-data retention process.

Google Calendar access

The Google account owner authorizes Vocalis to manage events on calendars they own. The deployed backend is configured to use only the dedicated Vocalis demo calendar. Public visitors do not sign in with Google or grant access to their calendars.

Questions

For questions or data removal requests, contact the project owner through the Vocalis GitHub repository. Do not include sensitive data in a public issue.
